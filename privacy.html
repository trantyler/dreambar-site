Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

DreamBar is a desktop dock and taskbar for Windows. It runs on your PC. The developer does not run any servers for DreamBar and does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal data.

Data stored on your PC

Your settings, pinned apps, notes, to-dos and logs are stored only on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenTaskbar. DreamBar has no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google in the Next Meeting widget, DreamBar asks Google for:

  • Your email address (openid, email), to show which account is signed in.
  • Read access to your calendars (calendar.readonly), to show your next meeting, today's agenda and a Join button for Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex links, and to list the calendars you can add events to.
  • Permission to manage events (calendar.events), so you can add an event, edit or delete events you organize, and answer invitations (Yes / Maybe / No) from the widget.

How this data is used:

  • DreamBar reads event titles, times, locations, descriptions (only to find a meeting link), video meeting links and your own response status, for events from one hour ago to the next 24 hours.
  • Event data is requested directly from Google by DreamBar on your PC, shown in the dock, and kept only in memory. It is never written to disk and never sent anywhere else.
  • DreamBar only creates, changes or deletes an event, or sends an invitation reply, when you do it yourself in the widget. It never changes your calendar on its own, and it cannot change calendar settings or sharing.
  • When you edit or delete an event that has guests, or answer an invitation, Google notifies the other people on that event, just as it does when you make the same change in Google Calendar.
  • DreamBar does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) for your Windows user account and stored only on your PC.
  • Signing out deletes the tokens from your PC and revokes DreamBar's access in your Google account. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

DreamBar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Other network requests

DreamBar only connects to the internet for features you turn on: the Weather widget (Open-Meteo), the Ping widget (the host you choose), AI usage widgets (your own provider accounts) and the Google Calendar sign-in above. These requests go straight from your PC to those services.

Children

DreamBar is not directed at children under 13.
