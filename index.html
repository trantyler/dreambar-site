DreamBar

A dock and taskbar for Windows 10 and 11, with live widgets and a Dynamic Island.

What it does

  • Replaces the Windows taskbar with a glass dock, and restores the normal taskbar whenever you quit.
  • Widgets for media, weather, system stats, focus timers, notes, to-dos, downloads and more.
  • A Dynamic Island at the top of the screen for volume, charging, downloads and your next meeting.

Google Calendar

The optional Next Meeting widget can sign in to your Google account to show your next meeting, today's agenda and a one-click Join button for meeting links. You can also add events, edit or delete your own, and answer invitations right from the widget. DreamBar only changes your calendar when you ask it to, and your calendar data stays on your PC. See the privacy policy for details.

DreamBar is based on OpenTaskbar by alztrk (MIT License).
